Privacy Policy

How Bak Bak handles information when you make one-to-one voice calls.

Effective 10 October 2026 India Adults 18+

Bak Bak is a voice-calling app that helps adults connect with another person for a direct, one-to-one voice conversation. This policy explains what information may be handled when you use Bak Bak and the choices available to you.

1. Who operates Bak Bak

Bak Bak is operated by AMS Solutions, an individual developer based in India. For privacy questions or requests about your information, contact ayushman.solutions999@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

Bak Bak is designed to collect limited information needed to provide its calling functionality. Based on the app configuration described for this policy, the following information may be handled:

  • Display name: The display name you provide or use in the app.
  • Voice and microphone: Bak Bak uses microphone access so you can speak during a live call. Calls are intended to be live and are not recorded or stored by Bak Bak, based on the current app design described to us.
  • Technical and diagnostic information: Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics may process app usage events, device/app information, and crash or diagnostic details to help understand app performance and fix problems. The exact fields depend on the SDK configuration.
  • Service and connection data: Firebase and LiveKit process information needed to operate the app, match callers, establish voice connections, and maintain service reliability. The exact records retained depend on configuration and provider practices.

Bak Bak is not intended to collect your precise location or access your phone contacts. It does not maintain a call-history feature, and it does not use advertising SDKs or display ads, based on the current app details provided for this policy.

3. Microphone and live calls

Microphone permission is needed to speak during a call. You can manage this permission through your device settings, but voice calling may not work if permission is denied.

LiveKit is used to support voice calling. Voice is transmitted to enable the live conversation. The app is described as not recording or storing call audio. However, network and service providers may process connection metadata or technical logs as part of delivering and securing the service. This policy does not make claims about provider-side logging beyond what has been verified.

4. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • Connect users for random, one-to-one voice calls.
  • Provide the app's core calling and display-name functionality.
  • Monitor reliability, diagnose crashes, and improve app performance.
  • Protect the service, investigate technical problems, and meet applicable legal obligations.

5. How information is shared

Information may be processed by service providers that help operate Bak Bak, including Firebase services and LiveKit, to the extent necessary for authentication or app operation, backend functions, analytics, crash diagnostics, and voice connections. These providers may process data under their own terms and privacy documentation.

Bak Bak does not display ads and is not described as selling personal information. This statement does not cover any data practices that may be introduced through future features or SDK changes; this policy should be updated if the app's practices change.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

6. Retention and security

Information associated with an account is intended to be retained until account deletion, subject to technical requirements, provider retention settings, backups, and any legal obligations. Some diagnostic or service-provider logs may have separate retention periods controlled by the relevant configuration or provider.

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Your choices and deletion requests

You can manage microphone access in your device settings. To request deletion of your account and associated personal information, email ayushman.solutions999@gmail.com from an address you can access and include “Bak Bak account deletion” in the subject. We may ask for reasonable information to verify the request.

Deletion may be subject to applicable legal requirements and limited technical retention in backups or security logs. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law and explain if any information must be retained.

8. Blocking and reporting

Blocking and reporting features are planned but are not represented here as currently available. Until these features are implemented, please end a call if you feel uncomfortable and contact us at ayushman.solutions999@gmail.com to raise a concern.

9. Age requirement

Bak Bak is intended for people aged 18 and older. It is not intended for children. If you believe a person under 18 has provided personal information through the app, contact us so we can review the concern and take appropriate action.

10. International processing

Service providers may process information in locations outside your state or country, depending on their infrastructure and configuration. Where applicable, such processing is subject to the providers' terms and the requirements of applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Bak Bak's features, providers, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when this version was prepared. Material changes will be reflected in an updated version, with additional notice where required by law.
